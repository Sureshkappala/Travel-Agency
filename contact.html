Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about booking, package inclusions, or trip customizations? Find quick answers below.

How do I book a trip? ?
Booking is simple! Explore our curated packages or destinations, select your desired dates and traveler count, and click "Book Now." You will receive an instant digital reservation reference and a dedicated concierge will confirm flight preferences within 2 hours.
Can I customize a package? ?
Absolutely. Every itinerary is fully customizable. You can adjust the number of days, upgrade to oceanfront villas, add private excursions, or combine multiple destinations by chatting with your assigned travel specialist.
Can I change my travel dates? ?
Yes! We offer flexible travel protection. You may reschedule your journey up to 14 days before departure with zero change penalty fees, subject to seasonal resort rate adjustments.
What is included in a package? ?
Our standard packages include handpicked 4-star or 5-star resort stays, private chauffeured transfers, licensed English-speaking local tour guides, entrance passes to all scheduled attractions, and daily gourmet breakfasts.
Do you provide hotel bookings? ?
Yes, our Hotels page lets you reserve standalone luxury stays, private villas, and boutique lodges worldwide with exclusive VIP perks like free room upgrades, breakfast, and resort spa credits.
How can I track my booking? ?
You can log into your Customer Dashboard anytime to view real-time countdown timers, downloadable flight vouchers, daily excursion itineraries, and direct contact info for your driver.
Can I cancel a booking? ?
Yes. Full 100% refunds are provided for cancellations made at least 7 days prior to departure for standard bookings. For customized luxury charters, our flexible travel insurance provides comprehensive coverage.
How can I contact travel support? ?
Our 24/7 global support team is accessible via WhatsApp, phone, email, and live portal messaging. When traveling, you also receive the direct mobile phone number of your local expedition manager.
