Bali, Indonesia

Island of Spiritual Temples, Emerald Terraces & Sun-drenched Coasts

★ 4.95 (1,420 Reviews)

Destination Overview

Known globally as the “Island of the Gods,” Bali enchants with its dramatic volcanic landscapes, iconic tiered rice paddies, ancient spiritual shrines, and azure coastlines. Whether seeking inner calm through yoga in Ubud, thrilling surf breaks on the Bukit Peninsula, or private luxury dining over cliffside oceans, Bali provides a profound, soul-nourishing escape.

Our travelers benefit from curated local access: private drivers fluent in English, exclusive cooking classes inside traditional Balinese compounds, skip-the-line passes at Uluwatu, and secluded beach club reservations.

Best Time to Visit April – October (Dry Season)
Local Currency Indonesian Rupiah (IDR)
Primary Airport Ngurah Rai (DPS), Denpasar
Languages Indonesian, Balinese, English

Top Attractions & Highlights

Tegallalang Rice Terraces

Walk through vibrant, cascading green valleys and experience the famous jungle swings.

Uluwatu Cliff Temple & Kecak Dance

Perched 70 meters above crashing waves, offering sunset fire dance performances.

Nusa Penida Kelingking Beach

A short speedboat journey to the iconic T-Rex shaped coastal headland and crystal bay.

Mount Batur Sunrise Trek

Hike an active volcano in pre-dawn hours to witness dawn break over the cloud deck.

Essential Travel Tips

  • 🔹 Temple Etiquette: Wear a sarong and sash when entering sacred temples (provided or rented at entrances).
  • 🔹 Getting Around: Private chauffeured cars are the safest, most comfortable way to navigate island traffic.
  • 🔹 Dining: Drink bottled water and explore both five-star dining and reputable traditional Warungs.
  • 🔹 Visa on Arrival: 30-day tourist visas are available upon landing for most international passport holders.
