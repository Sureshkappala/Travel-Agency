Bali Tropical Escape (5 Days / 4 Nights)

Private Pool Villa, Ubud Waterfalls, Volcanic Sunrises & Sunset Cliff Temples

★ 4.9 (340 Guest Reviews)

Tour Overview

Immerse yourself in Indonesia’s premier tropical haven. This carefully balanced 5-day tour gives you luxury, cultural immersion, and peaceful relaxation. You will stay in a private luxury pool villa nestled in the lush Ubud jungles before spending your final days along the vibrant southern coast.

Trip Code: ID-BAL-05
Group Size: Private (2-8 guests)
Pace: Relaxed / Moderate
Transport: Chauffeured SUV

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Click each day to reveal detailed schedules, meals, and included experiences.

Day 01 Welcome to Bali — VIP Airport Arrival & Ubud Villa Check-In
▼

Upon landing at Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS), you will be greeted by our airport concierge with chilled towels and refreshing tropical beverages. Transfer via private air-conditioned SUV to your boutique rainforest villa in Ubud. Enjoy a welcome traditional Balinese massage followed by an intimate 3-course dinner overlooking the Ayung River valley.

Meals: Welcome Dinner | Stay: Viceroy Ubud Luxury Private Pool Villa

Day 02 Ubud Sacred Monkey Forest & Tegallalang Rice Terraces
▼

Begin your morning with breakfast by your private infinity pool. At 9:00 AM, set out with your personal guide to the Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary. After lunch at an organic farm-to-table restaurant, hike the emerald tiers of Tegallalang Rice Terraces and try the famous canyon swing. The evening is yours to explore Ubud’s artisan craft markets.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch | Stay: Viceroy Ubud

Day 03 Mount Batur Sunrise Jeep Tour & Natural Thermal Springs
▼

Embark on an exhilarating early morning 4WD custom open-air Jeep excursion up the black lava fields of Mount Batur to witness the golden sunrise above the clouds. Afterward, soak in natural volcanic thermal hot springs overlooking Lake Batur before stopping at a sustainable Luwak coffee plantation.

Meals: Breakfast, Light Lunch | Stay: Viceroy Ubud

Day 04 Transfer to Seminyak & Sunset Uluwatu Temple Fire Dance
▼

Check out from Ubud and head south to the golden coastal sands of Seminyak. Check in to your oceanfront suite. In the late afternoon, drive to dramatic Uluwatu Temple perched atop a sheer 70-meter cliff. Experience the captivating Kecak Fire Dance performance against the glowing sunset, followed by a candlelit seafood barbecue on Jimbaran Beach.

Meals: Breakfast, Seafood BBQ Dinner | Stay: The Seminyak Beach Resort & Spa

Day 05 Leisure Beach Morning & Departure Chauffeur Transfer
▼

Enjoy a relaxed morning swimming or shopping for souvenirs. Your private driver will coordinate with your flight schedule for a stress-free departure transfer to DPS Airport.

Meals: Breakfast

🎒 Pre-Tour Packing & Climate Guide

Recommended packing essentials for maximum comfort during your 5-day escape.

✓
Lightweight Linen Clothing: Breathable fabrics for daytime tropical temperatures (27°C - 31°C).
✓
Warm Jacket for Mount Batur: Pre-dawn summit temperatures can drop to 14°C before sunrise.

Sturdy Walking Shoes: Grippy sneakers or trail sandals for rice terrace and waterfall hikes.
